JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

JARVIS is a personal dashboard that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is used solely by its owner.

Google data

With the owner's permission, JARVIS reads Gmail messages and Google Calendar events (read-only) to display them on the owner's private dashboard and send reminders to the owner's own devices. It never sends, edits, or deletes email or events.

Data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and local data is removed by deleting the app's data folder.
